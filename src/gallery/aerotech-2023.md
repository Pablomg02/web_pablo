My first year as team leader of UVigo Aerotech. This is the group photo from that season, when we were working on two aircraft at once, LIGN-0 and MOBULA-0. Their story is in the [XtraChallenge photo](../xtrachallenge-2023/).

We had one priority, above the results: that the whole team, not just a few of us, would get to travel to a competition and learn from it.
