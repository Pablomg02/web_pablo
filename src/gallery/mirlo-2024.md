Our entry for the 2024 Air Cargo Challenge, in Aachen. MIRL-0 had an unusual configuration and was built entirely from carbon fibre.

The results didn't live up to what the aircraft could really do, but we came home feeling that the team was growing stronger every year.

It was also, a little sadly, my last year as team leader of Aerotech.
