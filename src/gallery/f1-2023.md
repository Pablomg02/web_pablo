This photo is different from the rest: I'm not in it, I took it. The 2023 Spanish Grand Prix at the Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya, during the drivers' parade. On that truck rode the best drivers in the world. All of them brilliant, of course, but let's be honest: Verstappen and Alonso are on another level.

It is here for what it stands for. I love Formula 1: the competition, the sacrifice and everything it takes to get there. Above all, I admire the engineering behind it, put to work in the name of fair competition.
