The team's first competition: the 2022 Air Cargo Challenge, in Munich. The photo was taken once the flying was over.

CORV-0 took a fair number of knocks over those days and still came home in one piece. It was proof that we had overbuilt it. And on an aircraft, strength you don't need is paid for in weight.
